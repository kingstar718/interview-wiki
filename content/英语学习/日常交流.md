# 日常交流

等级 L3 · 每天 10 分钟 · 场景:寒暄→一、打招呼 & 寒暄,闲聊→二、聊天气（万能破冰话题）,请假→七、请假 & 迟到

> **每个场景一个模板，替换槽位即可。** 日常交流不需要高级词汇，需要的是"敢说"和"能接话"。小错误不影响沟通，不敢说才会。

---

## 学习地图

| 场景 | 核心原则 | 关键句 |
|------|---------|--------|
| 打招呼 | 简短 + 微笑 | How's it going? |
| 聊天气 | 万能破冰话题 | Can't believe it's [天气] today! |
| 聊周末 | 问做了什么，不说"没什么" | Did you get up to anything fun? |
| 聊美食 | 准备好介绍一道中餐 | It's kind of like [对方知道的菜], but [区别]. |
| 请假 | 直接说原因，不用过度解释 | I need to take a [半天/全天] off on [日期] for [原因]. |

## 一、打招呼 & 寒暄

### 见面

| 场景 | 说 | 回应 |
|------|-----|------|
| 早上 | Morning! / Good morning! | Morning! How are you? |
| 一般 | Hey, how's it going? | Good, you? / Not bad, busy week. |
| 周一 | How was your weekend? | It was great, I [做了什么]. How about you? |
| 周五 | Any plans for the weekend? | Not much, just relaxing. You? |
| 好久不见 | Long time no see! How have you been? | I know, right? Been busy with [项目]. |

### 常见回应（别只说 "I'm fine"）

| 对方说 | 你可以回 |
|--------|---------|
| How are you? | Pretty good, you? / Can't complain. / Busy but good! |
| How's it going? | It's going well! / Same old, same old. / Surviving! |
| How was your weekend? | It was great! I [做了什么]. / Pretty chill, just stayed home. |
| What's up? | Not much, just [正在做的事]. / Just finishing up [工作]. |

### 结束对话

| 场景 | 说 |
|------|-----|
| 去开会 | I'd better run — got a meeting in 5. Talk later! |
| 去吃饭 | I'm going to grab lunch. Want to join? / Catch you later! |
| 下班 | Alright, I'm heading out. See you tomorrow! |
| 周末 | Have a great weekend! |

## 三、聊周末 & 假期

### 问对方

| 说 | 语气 |
|-----|------|
| Did you get up to anything fun this weekend? | 轻松 |
| How was your trip to [地点]? | 关心 |
| Do you have any plans for the long weekend? | 期待 |
| Did you do anything special for [节日]? | 好奇 |

### 说自己

| 场景 | 说 |
|------|-----|
| 宅家 | I just stayed in and binge-watched [剧名]. |
| 出去玩 | I went hiking / checked out a new restaurant / visited [地点]. |
| 什么都没做 | Took it easy. Just recharged for the week. |
| 带孩子 | Chased my kids around all weekend. 😅 |
| 学习 | I was grinding [学什么]. Not fun but necessary. |

### 不会说"没什么"怎么办？

**中国人习惯说"没什么"，但英文里直接说"nothing"会让对话终结。** 加一点细节，哪怕很少：

| 不要说 | 要说 |
|--------|------|
| Nothing. | Not much, just [看了一部电影/睡了懒觉/做了顿饭]. |
| Just stayed home. | Just stayed home and caught up on some sleep. Felt great! |
| Nothing special. | Nothing too exciting. Just the usual — gym, groceries, Netflix. |

### 介绍中餐

**万能句式**：It's kind of like [对方知道的菜], but [区别].

| 中餐 | 怎么介绍 |
|------|---------|
| 火锅 | It's like a hot pot where you cook raw meat and veggies in boiling broth at the table. |
| 饺子 | Think of it as Chinese dumplings — dough wrapped around meat and veggies, boiled or pan-fried. |
| 宫保鸡丁 | It's diced chicken stir-fried with peanuts and chili — sweet, spicy, and savory. |
| 火锅 | You dip raw meat and veggies into a bubbling pot of spicy broth. It's a whole social experience. |
| 烤鸭 | It's roasted duck with crispy skin, served with thin pancakes, cucumber, and hoisin sauce. |
| 麻辣烫 | It's like a personalized hot pot — you pick your ingredients and they cook it in spicy broth. |
| 奶茶 | It's milk tea with chewy tapioca pearls at the bottom. Also called boba. |

## 七、请假 & 迟到

### 请假

**核心原则：直接说原因，不用过度解释。** 中文习惯铺垫一堆，英文直接说。

```
Hi [Manager],

I need to take [半天/全天] off on [日期] for [原因].
I've [做好交接安排].

Thanks!
```

| 原因 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 看医生 | I have a doctor's appointment. |
| 不舒服 | I'm not feeling well and need to take a sick day. |
| 家里有事 | I need to take a personal day. |
| 孩子生病 | My kid is sick — I need to stay home today. |
| 搬家 | I'm moving and need the day off. |
| 办证 | I have an appointment at the [机构]. |
| 修东西 | I have a repair person coming over. |

### 迟到

```
Hi team,

Running a bit late — [原因]. Should be in by [时间].
```

| 原因 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 堵车 | Traffic is terrible today. |
| 地铁故障 | The subway is delayed. |
| 起晚了 | Overslept. 😅 |
| 送孩子 | Dropping my kid off took longer than expected. |
| 有个急事 | Dealing with something urgent — be there soon. |

## 常见追问

### 口语不好，不敢开口怎么办？

**从"最小可开口"开始。** 不需要等到"英语够好了"再说。今天的目标不是流利对话，而是说出 **一句完整的话**。明天两句。后天三句。一个月后你会发现已经能聊 5 分钟了。关键是：**接受"会说但说不好"的阶段，这是必经之路。**

### 听不懂对方说什么怎么办？

| 说 | 场景 |
|-----|------|
| Sorry, could you say that again? | 没听清 |
| Could you slow down a bit? | 对方说话太快 |
| I didn't catch that last part. | 后半句没听清 |
| Sorry, what does [词] mean? | 有不认识的词 |
| Let me make sure I understand — you're saying [用自己的话复述]? | 想确认理解 |

### 不知道怎么接话？

**万能接话法**：
1. 重复对方最后几个词 + 问一个相关问题
2. 说 "That's interesting!" + 一个问题
3. 说 "Oh nice!" / "That's awesome!" + 分享自己的类似经历

**示例**：
- 对方："I went hiking last weekend."
- 你接："Hiking? Nice! Where did you go?" / "Oh nice! I've been wanting to go hiking too. Any trail recommendations?"
